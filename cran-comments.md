## Development draft — not a submission

The previous alsdownloader 0.1.3 submission was cancelled by CRAN at the
maintainer's request. The revised package is named ALSdownloadeR and is
currently version 0.1.3.9000 for local review.

reporting and comparison selection. See NEWS.md and docs/IMPLEMENTATION_REVIEW.md.

Record final version and current Windows, macOS, Linux and R-devel check
results here before a new submission. Results from the previous package do
not validate this revised implementation.
