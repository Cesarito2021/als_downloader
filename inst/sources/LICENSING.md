its browser bundles are not distributed in the R package.

## Data and access services

Keep the selected dataset's original citation, licence link, version/date and
any additional acknowledgement. See POLICIES.md for provider-specific evidence.
See USE_REVIEW.md for the 19 September 2026 access and acknowledgement audit,
including unresolved conditions for public hosting and source metadata gaps.
A public URL, a file format, scientific use, or use through this intermediary
does not establish permission. A citation alone cannot resolve missing rights.
Do not apply the software's GPL-3 or the screenshot library's MIT licence to
data from a provider. Metadata checks establish that fields are present, not
that the intended use has legal clearance.

OpenTopography requires compliance with the individual dataset licence and
attribution to both the source and OpenTopography. Its API account, key, rate
and commercial integration conditions also apply when using that service.
See https://opentopography.org/usageterms and
https://opentopography.org/node/10 . Do not share keys or bypass service limits.

## Figures and redistribution

PNG exports retain available dataset credits and licence links. Cloud figures
identify sampling/display transformations; map images retain basemap credits.
Local uploads have unknown terms unless supplied by their owner: the app cannot
infer a licence from LAS classification or intensity fields. Download the tile
metadata alongside figures and verify required source acknowledgements before
publication. A map's study-area boundary may have its own rights too.

An exported image is not automatically MIT or GPL-3 because those licences
apply to software used to make it. Check every included source and the basemap
provider's export/redistribution terms. For CC BY material, retain appropriate
credit and the licence link and identify modifications. Inclusion in the app
does not imply endorsement or guarantee clearance for a particular use.
